About DLS

Professional cleaning for London homes, tenants and businesses.

Who we are

DLS Professional Cleaning Services helps people across London and the surrounding area get a spotless result without the stress. Whether you are handing back the keys, moving into a new place, keeping your home fresh or looking after an office, we bring the people, products and equipment to do the job properly.

Our services include end of tenancy cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, deep cleaning, regular residential cleaning, office and commercial cleaning, and steam carpet and upholstery cleaning.

We keep things simple: clear prices, a friendly team, and a free re-clean if something is missed. New customers also get 20% off their first booking.
